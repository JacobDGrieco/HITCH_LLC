# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients, collaborators, and hiring teams evaluating the developer's creative direction and project work. They should be able to understand the portfolio quickly while the visual world communicates a distinctive point of view.

## Product Purpose

Present HITCH LLC and its portfolio as an explorable cloud scene, with clear routes to projects, experience, skills, contact information, and personal background. Success means the homepage feels memorable and handcrafted while remaining a practical navigation hub.

## Brand Personality

Dreamlike, grand, and meticulously crafted. The experience should feel cinematic and imaginative without making project content or navigation difficult to read.

## Anti-references

Generic card-grid portfolios, flat CSS panels pretending to have depth, repeated or visibly cropped cloud artwork, decorative glassmorphism with weak contrast, obvious gaps between windows and cloud frames, and oversized foreground clouds that hide project content.

## Design Principles

- Make the sky and cloud world the primary visual identity.
- Use real depth, overlap, and scale to establish hierarchy.
- Keep project content legible inside cohesive, substantial window frames.
- Let bespoke assets carry the fantasy instead of repeating one image.
- Preserve direct, accessible navigation despite the theatrical composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keep keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, readable text contrast, semantic route labels, and a reduced-motion experience that removes continuous floating and parallax.
